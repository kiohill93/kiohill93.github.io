Livedesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Livedesk is a desktop wallpaper app for Windows and macOS, made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what data Livedesk uses and where it goes.

In short: Livedesk runs entirely on your computer. We don't run servers for it, you don't make an account with us, and Livedesk sends us nothing: no analytics, no crash reports, no tracking. Your data goes only between your computer and the services you choose to connect.

How Livedesk works

Livedesk runs a small program on your computer that only your own computer can reach (127.0.0.1). It fetches what each card shows straight from the service involved, keeps it in memory to show it, and stores your settings on your computer. Nothing passes through us.

What each feature uses

FeatureWhat Livedesk reads or doesWho it talks to
Weather Your approximate location (from your internet address), unless you enter a location yourself. The location is used to get the forecast and to choose the night sky shown. Location lookup services (ipwho.is, ipapi.co) and weather providers (currently Open-Meteo, and in the US the National Weather Service)
Calendar Your events for the coming days, read-only. Livedesk never creates, changes or deletes events. Google Calendar, through a private calendar link you provide or by signing in with Google
To-do Your open tasks in Google Tasks. Livedesk adds a task when you ask it to and marks a task complete when you tick it. It never deletes tasks. Google Tasks, after you sign in with Google
Email (optional, advanced) For unread and starred messages: sender, subject and date only, read-only. Livedesk never downloads message bodies or attachments, never marks anything as read, and never sends email. Gmail, with an app password you create
News Headlines and links from the news sites and feeds you add. Clicking a headline opens it in your browser. The sites you add
Markets Your account balances, positions and watchlist, and market prices, read-only. Livedesk never places trades or moves money, and it doesn't give investment advice. Your broker (such as Alpaca or Charles Schwab), with API keys you create
Wallpaper Pictures you put in Livedesk's backgrounds folder are shown as the background. Nobody: they stay on your computer

Every connection is optional. A card you don't set up doesn't contact anything. Each service has its own privacy policy, which covers what it does with requests from your computer.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, Livedesk asks only for the permissions its cards need: Google Tasks (to show, add and complete tasks) and, for the Calendar card, read-only access to your calendars. Google user data is used only to show it on your own screen and to make the task changes you ask for. It is stored only on your computer, never sent to us or to anyone else, never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. No person reads it.

Livedesk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Livedesk's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party access settings.

What is stored, and where

Livedesk stores your settings, the news sources you add, and the sign-in tokens, app passwords and API keys you give it, on your computer only: in Livedesk's folder or your system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain). Card contents are kept in memory while Livedesk runs and are not saved. Livedesk keeps a local log file for troubleshooting, written so that it leaves out your calendar links, passwords and keys. Uninstalling Livedesk and deleting its folder removes all of it.

Licence keys and purchases

Livedesk checks its licence key on your computer, without contacting us. Purchases are handled by the store you buy from (itch.io or the Microsoft Store) under that store's privacy policy. We may receive the order details the store shares with sellers, such as an email address and an order number, and use them only to help with your purchase or licence.

Updates

On macOS, Livedesk checks this website for a newer version. That is an ordinary web request: it includes your internet address, as every web request does, and nothing about you or your data. On Windows, the Microsoft Store handles updates.

Children

Livedesk is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change affects how Google user data is handled, Livedesk will ask for your consent again before it applies.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact us through the Livedesk page on itch.io. A support email address will be listed here.
